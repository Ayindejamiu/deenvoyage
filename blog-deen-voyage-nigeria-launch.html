Deen Voyage Begins Operations in Nigeria, Targets Affordable Umrah and Hajj Services for Nigerian Muslims

June 17, 2026 By Deen Voyage Team 6 min read
Home  /  Blog  /  Deen Voyage Nigeria Launch

Canada-based Islamic travel company Deen Voyage Limited has officially launched operations in Nigeria, marking a significant expansion of its global footprint and signalling a new chapter for affordable, premium-quality pilgrimage services in the Nigerian market.

The company, which specialises in Umrah, Hajj, and Islamic tours, has established its Nigerian office in the Ikorodu area of Lagos, positioning the hub as a major operational centre for its activities across Africa. The development is expected to open up more competitive and globally benchmarked options for Nigerian Muslims seeking to perform Umrah and Hajj.

A New Option for Nigerian Pilgrims

Speaking during a media briefing to mark the launch, the company's Co-Founder, Mr. Yusuf Alimi, said Deen Voyage's entry into the Nigerian market is driven by a clear mission — to make spiritually enriching pilgrimages accessible to more Muslims without compromising on quality.

"Deen Voyage was founded with the goal of making spiritual journeys more comfortable, organised, and accessible. We understand the importance of Umrah and Hajj to Muslims, and we are committed to ensuring that every pilgrim experiences it with ease and dignity."

— Mr. Yusuf Alimi, Co-Founder, Deen Voyage Limited

He noted that Deen Voyage has already built a strong reputation for premium Umrah and Hajj packages in North America and Europe, and that Nigeria represents a natural and strategic next step in the company's growth.

Why Nigeria, and Why Now?

The timing of Deen Voyage's Nigerian launch is no coincidence. Demand for Umrah and Hajj services among Nigerian Muslims has continued to grow significantly in recent years, yet many intending pilgrims still face challenges around pricing transparency, service quality, and access to internationally competitive packages.

Industry observers noted that the company's arrival comes at a moment when the Nigerian Islamic travel market is ripe for fresh, reliable alternatives — particularly those that combine international standards with an understanding of the local pilgrim's needs.

Strategic Location With its base in Ikorodu, Lagos, Deen Voyage Nigeria is strategically positioned to serve pilgrims across Lagos and the wider South-West region, while also extending its reach to intending pilgrims in other parts of Nigeria.

Affordable Pricing, Premium Experience

One of the distinguishing propositions of Deen Voyage is its commitment to balancing affordability with quality — a combination that has historically been difficult to find in the Nigerian Umrah and Hajj travel space.

Mr. Alimi emphasised that the company's model is built around the belief that every Muslim deserves to perform their spiritual journey with comfort and dignity, regardless of their budget. The Nigerian office will offer curated Umrah packages, Hajj travel arrangements, and other Islamic tour services tailored to the specific needs and preferences of Nigerian pilgrims.

What's Included in Deen Voyage Packages Return flights · Hotel accommodation near the Haram · Umrah/Hajj visa · Daily meals · Expert-led Ziyarah visits to blessed sites in Mecca and Medina · Pre-departure orientation

From Canada to Lagos: A Global Company with Local Focus

Founded in Canada, Deen Voyage Limited has served pilgrims across North America and Europe, earning recognition for its organised, pilgrim-centred approach to Islamic travel. The company's expansion into Africa — beginning with Nigeria — reflects its ambition to grow into a truly global pilgrimage brand while maintaining a local, personalised touch in each market it enters.

The Lagos office is expected to serve not only as a booking and customer service centre but as a full operational hub, capable of coordinating travel logistics, documentation support, and pre-departure orientation for Nigerian pilgrims.

What This Means for the Nigerian Umrah and Hajj Market

Industry stakeholders have welcomed Deen Voyage's entry into Nigeria, noting that increased competition in the Islamic travel sector tends to benefit pilgrims through better pricing, improved service standards, and greater accountability from travel operators.

For Nigerian Muslims who have long sought a travel partner that combines international expertise with genuine care for the pilgrim experience, Deen Voyage's arrival presents a timely and credible option.
