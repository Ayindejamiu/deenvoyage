What Is the Qatar Hayya Visa?

The Qatar Hayya Visa is a short-stay entry visa that allows travellers — including Nigerians — to visit Qatar for tourism, transit, or leisure. It is processed through the official Discover Qatar partner network and approved by Qatar's Ministry of Interior (MOI).

The good news? Nigerian passport holders can obtain a Qatar visa, provided all requirements are met and the application is submitted through a registered travel agent like Deen Voyage Limited.

Qatar Hayya Visa Requirements for Nigerian Applicants (2026)

Before starting your application, make sure you have the following documents ready:

1
Valid International Passport
  • A colour scan of your passport's biographical data page (first page) is required.
  • Your passport must have a minimum of six (6) months validity from your planned date of arrival in Qatar.
  • Expired or soon-to-expire passports will not be accepted.
2
Recent Passport Photograph
  • The photo must be in JPEG or JPG format only — no PNG, PDF, or other formats.
  • Maximum file size: 5 MB.
  • Dimensions: 40mm in height by 30mm in width (standard passport photo size).
  • Ensure the photo is recent, clear, and taken against a plain white or light background.
3
Confirmed Hotel Booking in Qatar (Minimum 3 Nights)
  • You must have a confirmed hotel reservation for at least three (3) nights.
  • The booking must be made through the Discover Qatar agent portal — bookings made directly on the Discover Qatar B2C (public) website are not accepted for visa purposes.
  • This is why using a registered Discover Qatar partner like Deen Voyage Limited is essential — we book directly through the agent portal on your behalf.
4
Confirmed Airport Transfers
  • A confirmed Airport → Hotel → Airport transfer must be in place and submitted alongside your hotel booking.
5
Confirmed Flight Tickets
  • A copy of your confirmed return flight ticket to and from Qatar is required.
  • Your travel dates must align with your hotel booking and visa application window.

Important Things Every Nigerian Applicant Must Know

Visa Processing Time

The Qatar Hayya Visa requires a minimum of five (5) working days to process. Note that Fridays and Saturdays are weekends in Qatar and are not counted as working days. Plan your application well in advance — do not apply at the last minute.

Important Notice Do not apply at the last minute. The 5 working-day processing window excludes Fridays and Saturdays (Qatar's weekend). Late applications risk denial or delays that disrupt your travel plans.

Non-Refundable Fees

Hotel booking fees and visa administration fees are non-refundable once used to process the visa — regardless of whether the visa is approved or not. If your visa is not approved, the visa admin fee will not be refunded. In that case, we strongly advise you to immediately follow your hotel's cancellation policy to avoid No Show charges.

Visa Approval Is Not Guaranteed

All Hayya Visa approvals are strictly subject to the Ministry of Interior (MOI) of Qatar. Submitting correct documents significantly improves your chances, but ultimate approval rests with the MOI. Deen Voyage will guide you to ensure your application is as strong as possible.

Stay Where You Booked

Qatar's visa policy requires that travellers stay at the hotel booked for the visa application. Changing your accommodation after obtaining the visa is a violation of the visa policy and can have serious consequences for your travel agent. As your agent, Deen Voyage takes this seriously — please honour your booking as submitted.

Policy Reminder You must stay at the exact hotel booked during the visa application process. Any change of accommodation after visa approval violates Qatar's entry conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Nigerians get a Qatar visa?
Yes. Nigerian passport holders can obtain a Qatar Hayya Visa when they apply through a registered Discover Qatar travel partner with all required documents.
How long does the Qatar visa take to process from Nigeria?
A minimum of five (5) working days, excluding Fridays and Saturdays (Qatar's weekend days). Always apply well in advance of your travel date.
What happens if my Qatar visa is not approved?
The visa admin fee is non-refundable. However, you should immediately check your hotel's cancellation policy to avoid No Show charges. Deen Voyage will guide you through next steps.
Do I need a hotel booking to get a Qatar visa?
Yes. A confirmed hotel booking of at least three (3) nights — made through the Discover Qatar agent portal — is mandatory for the Hayya Visa. You cannot use a direct booking from the public Discover Qatar website.
Can I use the Qatar visa for Umrah transit?
Yes, many Nigerian pilgrims use Qatar/Doha as a transit point while travelling to Madinah or Makkah via Qatar Airways. Deen Voyage can package your Umrah trip together with your Qatar stopover visa.
